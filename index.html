常见问题 FAQ

需要什么设备和系统？

iPhone 或 iPad，系统需要 iOS / iPadOS 18 或更新版本。「用大白话解释」用的是设备上的 Apple 智能模型，需要 iOS 26 和支持 Apple 智能的设备；其他功能不受影响。

An iPhone or iPad running iOS / iPadOS 18 or later. "Explain it plainly" uses Apple's on-device model and needs iOS 26 on a device that supports Apple Intelligence; everything else works without it.

界面和正文能换语言吗？

能。「我的 → 设置 → 语言」里，界面语言可以选中文或 English；阅读语言可以选中文、English 或中英对照。读的时候也能在阅读器工具栏里随时切换，单语模式下点一段文字可以看它的另一种语言。

Yes. In Me → Settings → Language, choose Chinese or English for the interface, and Chinese, English or side-by-side for the book text. You can also switch from the reader's toolbar; in single-language mode, tap a paragraph to see it in the other language.

换了手机或重装后，怎么恢复完整版？

打开「我的 → 设置 → 完整版 → 恢复购买」，用当初购买时的 Apple 账户登录 App Store 即可，不会重复扣费。

Open Me → Settings → Full Book → Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. You won't be charged again.

家人可以一起用吗？

可以，完整版支持「家人共享」。在系统设置里开启家人共享和「购买项目共享」后，家庭成员在自己的设备上就能使用；如果没有马上解锁，点一下「恢复购买」。

Yes — the Full Book supports Family Sharing. With Family Sharing and Purchase Sharing turned on, family members can use it on their own devices; if it doesn't unlock right away, tap Restore Purchases.

阅读进度和笔记保存在哪里？能备份吗？

阅读进度、书签、高亮、笔记、练习、术语卡、小测和实验记录只保存在你这台设备上。「我的 → 设置 → 数据 → 导出学习数据」会生成一个 JSON 文件，可以存到「文件」App 或发给自己。删除 App 会删掉这些记录。

Reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, exercises, flashcards, quizzes and lab records are stored only on this device. Me → Settings → Data → Export learning data saves them as a JSON file you can keep in Files or send to yourself. Deleting the app deletes these records.

「用大白话解释」会把我读的内容发到网上吗？

不会。它用的是设备上的 Apple 智能模型，在本机生成解释，文字不离开你的设备，也不需要联网。设备不支持时，这个按钮不会出现。

No. It runs Apple's on-device model, so the explanation is generated on your device; nothing is sent anywhere and no connection is needed. On devices that don't support it, the button doesn't appear.

怎么申请退款？

购买和付款都由 Apple 处理，开发者看不到订单，也无法直接退款。请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 登录你的 Apple 账户申请。

Purchases are handled by Apple; we can't see orders or issue refunds. Please request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
